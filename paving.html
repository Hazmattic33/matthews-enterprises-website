What We Do
New Asphalt Paving

Whether you need a brand new driveway, a repaved parking lot, or an expanded access road, Matthew's Enterprises delivers smooth, durable asphalt built to handle New Hampshire's harsh winters and heavy use.

We handle every phase of the job — grading and base preparation, proper drainage, and a hot-mix asphalt surface applied to the correct depth for the application. We don't cut corners on base work, because that's what determines how long your pavement lasts.

A new asphalt driveway dramatically improves curb appeal and adds value to your home. We install driveways of all sizes, from single-car to long country driveways, using full-depth construction that handles vehicle loads and freeze-thaw cycles without cracking prematurely.

Commercial Paving

Benefits of Asphalt Paving

Durability

Properly installed asphalt lasts 20–30 years with regular maintenance.

Cost-Effective

Asphalt is typically less expensive to install than concrete and easier to repair.

Winter Performance

Asphalt's dark surface absorbs heat and melts ice faster than concrete.

Curb Appeal

Common Questions
FAQ

How long does a new asphalt driveway take?

Most residential driveways are completed in one day. Larger commercial projects may take 2–3 days. The surface can typically be driven on within 24–48 hours of installation.

How thick should my asphalt driveway be?

Residential driveways typically use 2–3 inches of compacted asphalt over a properly prepared aggregate base. Commercial lots handling heavy vehicles may require 4 or more inches.

Do I need a permit for a new driveway?

Permit requirements vary by municipality. We can advise you on local requirements in Claremont and surrounding NH towns based on your specific project.

Can you pave over my existing gravel driveway?

Yes, in many cases. We assess the existing base condition and grade before paving. A solid, properly graded gravel base is often sufficient to pave over without full excavation.
