What We Do
Hot-Pour Crack Sealing

Cracks in asphalt are inevitable, but leaving them untreated is a costly mistake. Water enters cracks, freezes in winter, and expands — turning a minor crack into a pothole that requires far more expensive repairs. Crack sealing is the most effective way to extend pavement life and prevent that cycle from starting.

Matthew's Enterprises uses hot-pour rubberized crack filler, which is the professional-grade method used on highways and commercial lots. It bonds directly to the asphalt edges, remains flexible through freeze-thaw cycles, and doesn't shrink or pop out like cold-pour fillers sold at hardware stores.

When to Seal Cracks

Crack sealing is most effective on cracks that are between 1/4 inch and 1.5 inches wide. We recommend treating cracks as soon as you notice them — ideally in spring or fall when temperatures are moderate. Wider or deeper damage may indicate a need for patching or overlay work, which we can also assess and quote.

Common Questions
FAQ

How long does crack sealing last?

Hot-pour crack sealant typically lasts 3–5 years before needing reapplication. Paired with regular sealcoating, treated pavement can last significantly longer.

Can all cracks be sealed?

Most cracks can be sealed. Very wide cracks (over 1.5 inches) or areas with alligator cracking may require patching or overlay rather than crack sealing — we'll advise you honestly at the estimate.

How soon can I use my driveway after crack sealing?

Hot-pour sealant cures quickly — typically within 30–60 minutes in warm weather. We'll let you know the exact wait time based on conditions on the day of the job.
