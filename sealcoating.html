What We Do
Professional Asphalt Sealcoating

Asphalt sealcoating is one of the most cost-effective ways to protect your driveway or parking lot. Without a protective seal, asphalt is exposed to UV rays, rain, oil, and freeze-thaw cycles that break it down from the surface in. Sealcoating creates a barrier that slows that deterioration, restores color, and can double the lifespan of your pavement.

At Matthew's Enterprises, we apply commercial-grade coal tar or asphalt-based sealcoat using professional squeegee and spray equipment to ensure even coverage and a clean, finished look.

Our Sealcoating Process

We start with a thorough cleaning of the surface — blowing out cracks, removing debris, and treating oil spots. Any existing cracks are filled before we apply the sealcoat, so you're getting a complete treatment, not just a cosmetic coating. We apply two coats for maximum protection and let the surface cure fully before opening it to traffic.

Common Questions
FAQ

How often should I sealcoat my driveway?

For residential driveways, we recommend sealcoating every 2–3 years. Commercial parking lots may benefit from annual or biennial treatment depending on traffic volume.

How long does sealcoating take to dry?

Sealcoating typically takes 24–48 hours to fully cure. We recommend keeping vehicles off the surface for at least 24 hours, and longer in cooler or humid weather.

What is the best time of year to sealcoat?

Late spring through early fall is ideal — temperatures should be above 50°F and no rain expected for 24 hours after application.

Do you sealcoat commercial parking lots?

Yes. We handle both residential driveways and commercial parking lots of all sizes throughout the Claremont, NH area.
